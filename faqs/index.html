Help & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about our services, pricing, timelines, reporting and how we work with clients.

1 What makes Mediamozak different from traditional agencies?

General

Mediamozak focuses on measurable business outcomes rather than vanity metrics. We integrate advanced web architecture with direct-response performance ad funnels.

2 Which services does Mediamozak offer?

Services

We offer web development, SEO, performance marketing, social media marketing, e-commerce marketing, quick commerce marketing and lead generation.

3 How long does it take to see results from SEO vs Performance Marketing?

Services

Performance marketing can deliver leads and traffic within 24–48 hours of campaign launch, whereas SEO produces compounding long-term organic growth over 3 to 6 months.

4 Do you offer customized retainer models?

Pricing

Yes! We tailor our retainer models to your specific growth stage—whether you need project-based web development or an all-inclusive growth performance package.

5 How much do your services cost?

Pricing

Pricing depends on the service, the scope of work and how competitive your market is. After a free strategy call we share a written proposal listing exactly what is included. Ad spend is separate from our fee.

6 What is Quick-com Marketing and how can my brand benefit?

Quick-Com

Quick-com marketing targets fast delivery apps like Blinkit and Zepto. We optimize your product visibility inside these apps to capture high-intent buyers in real time.

7 Where is Mediamozak located and which areas do you serve?

General

Our office is in Hari Nagar, West Delhi 110064, and we are open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM IST. We work with businesses across Delhi NCR and the rest of India.

8 Who owns the ad accounts, analytics and website?

Working Together

You do. Ad accounts, Google Analytics, Search Console and your website belong to your business, so your data and history stay with you.

9 How do you measure and report results?

Reporting

We report in business numbers: leads, cost per lead, sales and return on ad spend. Because many enquiries in India arrive by phone and WhatsApp, we track those as well as form submissions.

10 Can you guarantee first-page rankings on Google?

SEO

No. Google itself says nobody can guarantee a ranking. What we commit to is a clear plan, transparent reporting and steady work on the factors that improve rankings. Our 90-day SEO plan shows what that looks like.

11 Should I start with Google Ads or Meta Ads?

Ads

Google Ads captures people who are already searching for what you sell, while Meta Ads creates demand among people who aren't. Our guide to Google Ads vs Meta Ads explains how to choose.

12 Do you build SEO-ready websites?

Web

Yes. Every website we build includes on-page SEO: fast loading, mobile-friendly layouts, clean titles and meta descriptions, proper headings and schema markup.
